I'm Mrudhuhas, a machine learning engineer based in India with about eight years of experience across classical machine learning, deep learning, NLP and, more recently, large language models.

My focus now is reinforcement learning for LLM post-training: instruction tuning, reward modeling, preference learning, policy optimization and RL with verifiable rewards. Alongside that I work on the systems side — GPU programming with CUDA and Triton, PyTorch internals and distributed training.

I like to understand things from first principles: derive the method, implement it, measure it on real hardware, and write up what I learn. The [projects](/projects/) and [writing](/writing/) on this site are that process in public. My older notebooks are on [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/mrudhuhas).

I'm open to remote work with teams in the US and Europe — full-time, contract or part-time. The best way to reach me is [email](mailto:mrudhuhas@gmail.com).
